# CL-003 — Video Upload and Publishing Checklist

## Upload

- [ ] **"+"** button opens the creation menu
- [ ] Video can be selected from the gallery
- [ ] Selected video preview is displayed
- [ ] Upload progress is visible
- [ ] App does not enter "Reconnecting..." on a stable connection
- [ ] Upload failure shows a clear error and retry option

## Publishing and Visibility

- [ ] Published video appears on the user's profile
- [ ] Visibility settings are applied
- [ ] Comments and reactions are available on the published video

## Edge Cases

- [ ] Large video file
- [ ] Unsupported video format
- [ ] Network loss during upload
- [ ] App sent to background during upload
