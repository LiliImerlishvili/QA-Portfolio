## Kitchenware & Home Organization Platform (NDA Project)

**Role:** Manual QA Engineer  
**Period:** Nov 2025  

### Project Overview
Manual QA testing for a B2C e-commerce platform focused on kitchenware and home organization products, ensuring stable shopping journeys, consistent UI/UX, and accurate analytics tracking across devices.

### Responsibilities & Achievements
- Performed manual testing of navigation menus, category pages, popups, banners, and product detail pages
- Identified and reduced UI/UX inconsistencies, improving overall visual stability and usability
- Validated end-to-end shopping journeys, including inventory and availability flows
- Conducted cross-browser and responsive testing on Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge
- Verified cookie consent behavior and analytics accuracy (GA4 / GTM)
- Reported and tracked defects in Jira and supported retesting cycles before releases

### Testing Type
Functional · Regression · UI/UX · Cross-browser

### Tools Used
Jira · Chrome DevTools · Postman · GA4 · GTM · TestRail
