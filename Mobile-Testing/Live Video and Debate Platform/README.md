# Live Video and Debate Platform

Confidential mobile (iOS) and web platform focused on user-generated video content, live debates and real-time user interaction. Role: **Manual QA Engineer**.

## Challenge

The platform includes complex multi-user flows involving participants, moderators and viewers, with functionality depending on user role, session status, timing and permissions. Critical areas include authentication, live sessions, video publishing, notifications and account management.

## Approach

- Tested mobile and web end-to-end user flows
- Verified registration, authentication and social login scenarios
- Tested live debate creation, scheduling and publishing
- Validated participant, moderator and viewer permissions
- Tested invitations, pre-room, countdown and reconnect scenarios
- Verified video recording, upload, publishing and visibility settings
- Tested comments, reactions, notifications and user profiles
- Performed positive, negative, edge-case and exploratory testing
- Prepared structured checklists for reusable regression coverage
- Performed accessibility testing with VoiceOver and Appium Inspector
- Reported defects with clear steps, expected/actual results and evidence

## Key Findings

- Authentication and account-flow issues
- Role and permission inconsistencies
- Scheduling, timer and session-state issues
- Video upload and publishing defects
- Validation and notification issues
- Mobile UI/UX and accessibility problems
- Regression issues after updates and fixes

## Impact

Improved coverage and reliability of critical mobile/web flows, strengthened regression testing, and helped identify usability, accessibility and functional issues before release.
