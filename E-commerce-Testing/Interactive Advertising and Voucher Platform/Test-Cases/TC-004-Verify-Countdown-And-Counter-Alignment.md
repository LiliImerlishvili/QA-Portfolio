# TC-004 — Verify Countdown and Counter Alignment

## Related Defect

- BR-004

## Objective

Verify consistent baseline alignment across mobile and tablet.

## Preconditions

- Countdown is enabled.
- Download and view counters are visible.

## Test Steps

1. Open a cube containing countdown and counters.
2. Switch to mobile preview.
3. Compare the vertical alignment.
4. Repeat on tablet preview.

## Expected Result

Countdown and counters share the same visual axis without overlap.
