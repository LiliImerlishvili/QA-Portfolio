# TC-006 — Verify Voucher CTA Visual Consistency

## Related Defect

- BR-006

## Objective

Verify that voucher CTAs use consistent styling across card and popup.

## Preconditions

- Voucher download is enabled.
- Both card CTA and popup CTA are visible.

## Test Steps

1. Open a mobile campaign card.
2. Review the Download Voucher CTA.
3. Open the voucher popup.
4. Compare CTA colors, border, typography, and state behavior.

## Expected Result

Colors, border, typography, and interaction states are consistent.

Not Executed
