# Kitchenware and Home Organization Platform

**Role:** Manual QA Engineer  
**Platform:** Web  
**Period:** Nov 2025  

## Overview

B2C e-commerce platform for kitchenware and home organization products, focused on stable shopping journeys, consistent UI/UX and accurate analytics across devices.

## Testing Focus

- Catalog navigation, search and product pages
- Add to cart, cart management and checkout validation
- Out-of-stock and low-stock handling
- Localization and language switching
- Promotions, popups and banners
- Cookie consent and GA4 / GTM analytics

## Testing Types

Functional · Regression · UI/UX · Cross-browser
