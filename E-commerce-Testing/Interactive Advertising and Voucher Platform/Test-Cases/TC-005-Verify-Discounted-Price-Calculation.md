# TC-005 — Verify Discounted Price Calculation

## Related Defect

- BR-005

## Objective

Verify that the discounted price is calculated accurately.

## Test Data

- Original price: 200
- Discount: 25%
- Expected discounted price: 150

## Preconditions

- Price and discount fields are available.
- A cube with a discount offer exists.

## Test Steps

1. Open the discount configuration.
2. Enter an original price.
3. Enter a discount percentage.
4. Save the cube.
5. Review the calculated discounted price.

## Expected Result

The system displays 150 as the discounted price.
