# AI-Powered E-commerce Marketplace

Web-based e-commerce platform that enables users to create and manage online stores, publish products and sell through a centralized marketplace. Role: **Manual QA Engineer**.

## Challenge

The platform combines seller-side store management with customer-facing marketplace functionality. Changes across store configuration, products, categories, discounts, authentication and checkout could affect both merchants and customers.

## Approach

- Tested registration, login and social authentication flows
- Validated AI-assisted store creation and onboarding
- Tested store settings, business information and account management
- Verified product creation, editing, categories and attributes
- Tested reusable templates and category assignment
- Validated discounts, expiration rules and pricing scenarios
- Tested storefront navigation, product details and reviews
- Verified cart and checkout functionality
- Tested customer management and seller-side workflows
- Validated reporting and file export functionality
- Tested image upload and account-setting validations
- Performed positive, negative and edge-case testing
- Investigated frontend, network and authentication issues using DevTools
- Performed regression testing after fixes and updates
- Reported defects with clear steps and supporting evidence

## Key Findings

- Authentication and registration issues
- Validation and input-handling defects
- Product, category and template inconsistencies
- Store configuration and navigation issues
- Discount and pricing-related defects
- Checkout and storefront UI issues
- Error-handling and regression issues

## Impact

Improved reliability across seller and customer journeys, strengthened regression coverage and helped identify functional, validation and usability issues across core e-commerce workflows.
